Power BI

HR Attrition & Workforce Dashboard

Who is leaving, and where should HR focus retention effort?

What it shows

  • Attrition is 16.1% (711 of 4,410 employees) and skews young. Employees aged 18–25 leave at about 36% (132 of 369), roughly four times the rate for ages 36–45 (about 9%).
  • Three roles account for 63% of leavers. Sales Executive (165), Research Scientist (159) and Laboratory Technician (126) make up 450 of the 711.
  • Department size explains the department gap. R&D loses the most people (453), but its rate of about 16% is close to Sales at 15%.

What I would recommend

Aim retention at early-career staff in those three roles, with structured onboarding, clearer progression and early check-ins, instead of department-wide programmes.

Tableau

Customer Churn Dashboard

Which customers are leaving, and where would retention effort pay off most?

What it shows

  • 26.54% of 7,043 customers churned, putting $139,131 of $456,117 in monthly revenue (about 30%) at risk.
  • Tenure is the strongest signal. Churn is 47.4% in the first 12 months and falls to 9.5% for customers with 49 or more months.
  • Contract and service type stand out. Month-to-month contracts make up about 1,650 of the 1,869 churned customers, and fiber optic customers churn at 41.9% versus 19.0% on DSL.

What I would recommend

Start with first-year, month-to-month customers: offer incentives to move to annual contracts, and review fiber optic service quality, rather than sending blanket discounts.

Power BI

Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard

Where is the supply chain exposed to stockouts and forecasting misses?

What it shows

  • Revenue is $33.4M with an average margin of 32.4%, split almost evenly across four regions at $8.3M–$8.4M each.
  • Stockout risk is about 5.5% at every warehouse (5.47%–5.58%), which points to a network-wide issue rather than one weak site.
  • Forecast accuracy drops from 91.5% in April to 47.7% in October, and it is weakest in the low-demand months from August to November.

What I would recommend

Fix the second-half forecast first, since that is where accuracy loses about 40 points, and review safety stock across the whole network to address the flat 5.5% stockout risk.

About

About

I'm a data analyst based in Karachi, working mainly in Power BI and Tableau, with SQL and Excel for the groundwork underneath. I like taking a pile of messy, disconnected data and turning it into something a manager can actually look at and make a call on.

Most of my recent work has been building end-to-end dashboards — from cleaning the source data to designing the visuals — across sales performance, HR attrition, financial reporting, marketing campaigns and customer churn. I'm currently learning Python and exploring AI and automation tools, so I can spend less time on repetitive reporting work and more time on the analysis that actually matters.
